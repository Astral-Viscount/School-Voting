Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

School Voting is a student-built web app for running school prefect elections.

1. Who runs this site

School Voting is built and run by Mahi Mahatab, a student.

2. What I get from Google when you sign in

You sign in with your Google account using Google Sign-In. The only information I receive and use is:

  • your name
  • your email address (and whether Google has verified it)
  • your Google account ID, an anonymous identifier that lets us recognise you next time

I use these only to check you are allowed to use the site, to create your account, and to show your name on your own dashboard.

School Voting's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy.

3. What else I store

  • Your account: the name, email and Google ID above, whether you are an administrator, and when your account was created.

4. How I use your information

  • to confirm you are allowed to sign in (an approved school email domain, or an administrator)

I do not use your information for advertising, I do not sell it, and I do not use it to profile you.

5. Other services involved

  • Google handles sign-in. Google's own privacy policy applies to that step.
  • PythonAnywhere hosts the site and stores its database. Servers may be located outside New Zealand.

6. Cookies

I use one session cookie to keep you signed in (it expires after 8 hours) and a security token to protect forms from forgery. Neither is used for tracking or advertising.

7. Security

The site uses HTTPS, verifies Google sign-in tokens on the server, restricts every page by role.

8. Changes

I may update this policy, for example when new features are added. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

9. Contact

Questions, access or deletion requests: md.mahatabmahimn@gmail.com
